# Authors

TempLat began as the C++ engine of [CosmoLattice](https://cosmolattice.net) and
was released as a standalone library in 2026. The people listed here are the
authors of TempLat, referred to collectively as "The TempLat authors" in
[`LICENSE.md`](LICENSE.md).

| Author | Period | Contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wessel Valkenburg | 2021 | Initial design and implementation |
| Adrien Florio | 2021– | Main contributor |
| Franz R. Sattler | 2025– | Main contributor; Kokkos externalization and C++20 rewrite |
| Jorge Baeza-Ballesteros | 2022– | Maintenance, testing, and matrix algebra |

Per-file attribution is recorded in the `Main contributor(s):` line in the header
of each source file.

## Origin

TempLat was developed as part of CosmoLattice, released under the MIT license by
Daniel G. Figueroa, Adrien Florio, Francisco Torrentí and Wessel Valkenburg. The
CosmoLattice physics layer, CosmoInterface, is maintained separately and is not
part of this repository.
